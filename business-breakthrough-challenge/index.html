The Pusher

When growth stalls, you add hours. More output. More pressure. But the harder you push, the more exhausted you feel — because you’re trying to outwork a problem that doesn’t respond to effort.

The Thinker

When growth stalls, you think harder. Rewrite the offer. Tweak the funnel. You have a Notion doc called “2026 Strategy v7.” It feels strategic. But underneath it, thinking has become the place you go to avoid the risk of moving.

The Dreamer

When growth stalls, you reach for the next vision. New idea. New direction. But before one thing has time to work, you’re already onto something else.
