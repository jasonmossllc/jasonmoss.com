Questions

Before you join.

Is this right for me?

It’s built for coaches, consultants, and service-based business owners who have worked with paying clients, have an offer they believe in, and want more chances to sell it, without adding more to their plate.

It’s not the right fit if you’re still deciding what to sell, haven’t built relationships in your field yet, or want a script to blast your contact list. If that’s you, those foundations come first.

Will I actually sign a client in seven days?

That’s the goal we’ll work toward. It isn’t guaranteed.

Your offer, your relationships, your follow-through, and other people’s timing all play a role.

What we’ll do together is concrete: find your opportunities, write your outreach, and practice the conversations that lead to paying clients. You’ll leave with a process you can keep using long after the sprint.

What if I don’t have a big audience?

You don’t need thousands of followers. You need people who already know you or your work.

That could be past clients, old inquiries, professional connections, email subscribers, or people who regularly engage with your content.

Inside the sprint, we’ll help you find those people and decide who to reach out to first.

Does this involve cold DMs or pitching my friends?

No. You won’t be blasting your contact list or trying to sell coaching to everyone you know.

You’ll learn how to spot the people your work could genuinely help, make a relevant invitation, and explore working together when there’s a real fit… without forcing the conversation.

What if I can’t attend live?

You’ll get lifetime access to every recording, including the coaching call.

Attending live lets you ask questions and practice with us. If you miss a session, catch the replay… and come back to it whenever you need a refresher.
